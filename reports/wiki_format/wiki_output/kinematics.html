Kinematics

Kinematics is the branch of mechanics that describes motion purely geometrically --- positions, velocities, and accelerations --- without asking why the object moves.

Key Quantities

QuantitySymbolUnit
Position$\vec{r}$m
Velocity$\vec{v} = \dot{\vec{r}}$m/s
Acceleration$\vec{a} = \ddot{\vec{r}}$m/s$^2$

Equations of Motion (Constant Acceleration)

For uniform acceleration $a$:

\[ v = v_0 + at \]

\[ x = x_0 + v_0 t + \tfrac{1}{2} a t^2 \]

\[ v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a(x - x_0) \]

Projectile Motion

A classic application: under gravity $g$, horizontal and vertical motion are independent. The trajectory is a parabola.

\[ x(t) = v_0 \cos\theta \cdot t, \quad y(t) = v_0 \sin\theta \cdot t - \tfrac{1}{2} g t^2 \]
