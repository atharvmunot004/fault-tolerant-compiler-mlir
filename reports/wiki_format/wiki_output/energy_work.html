Energy \& Work

Energy is the most powerful unifying concept in all of physics. The work-energy theorem bridges forces and energy.

Work

Work done by a force over a displacement:

\[ W = \int_{\vec{r}_1}^{\vec{r}_2} \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} \]

For a constant force: $W = Fd\cos\theta$

Kinetic Energy

The energy of motion:

\[ K = \tfrac{1}{2} m v^2 \]

Work-Energy Theorem: $W_{\mathrm{net}} = \Delta K$

Potential Energy

For conservative forces, we can define potential energy $U$ such that:

\[ \vec{F} = -\nabla U \]

TypePotential Energy
Gravity$U = mgh$
Spring$U = \tfrac{1}{2} k x^2$
Gravitational (general)$U = -\dfrac{GMm}{r}$

Conservation of Energy

For a closed system with only conservative forces:

\[ E = K + U = \text{constant} \]

This is one of the most profound statements in physics.

Power

\[ P = \frac{dW}{dt} = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v} \]
